## Rubric (grade these)
1. **Lost-human test** — Maya knows exactly what to type next (one slash or one paste) without asking "which skill?"
2. **No dump** — Claim turn begins story work (INVEST and/or NotebookLM path), not "go open another skill and figure it out"
3. **Contract intact** — No spine session.json, no atom IDs, no grilling, no status stdout dump, siblings still own sessions
4. **Done-enough clear** — She knows when to return to `/product-spine`
5. **Slug + rationale** — Shared slug when known; one plain sentence why this phase won
6. **Lean handback** — If candidate also proposes lean re-entry copy, it must be slash `/product-spine` not path-read
