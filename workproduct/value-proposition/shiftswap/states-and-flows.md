# States and flows — ShiftSwap

Critical paths only. Weight Empty and Error — sampling dies there.

## Primary job flow

- (fact) Get a trusted coworker to cover my shift so I keep my job standing and the floor stays staffed.
- (decision) Priority job: get a trusted coworker to cover my shift tonight without group-chat chaos — high importance, immediate trigger, current alternatives leave them dissatisfied.


## Trigger and start

- (fact) Trigger: a coworker texts or posts in the group chat that they need coverage for tonight or tomorrow because of illness, childcare, or a better tip night elsewhere.


## Pain points on the path

- (hypothesis) Group-chat chaos (scroll, @everyone noise, nobody commits). Risk of double-booking or no-show. Fear of looking flaky to the manager. Time spent chasing replies while already on the floor.
- (hypothesis) Post+confirm replaces noisy group-chat threads (chaos pain). Confirm+notify reduces no-show / double-book risk. Manager ping cuts the second chase and flaky-look fear. Structured request cuts floor-time chasing replies.


## Desired outcomes

- (hypothesis) A confirmed cover in one place. Clear who is taking which hours. Manager notified without a second chase. Faster yes/no so they can stop refreshing the chat.
- (hypothesis) Confirm flow creates a single clear yes/no and named cover (desired gain). Manager ping creates notified-without-chase. Eligible-coworker list creates faster resolution so they stop refreshing chat.
