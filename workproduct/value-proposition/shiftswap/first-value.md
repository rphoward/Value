# First value — ShiftSwap

What "it worked" looks like in the first session. Hide orphans and secondary jobs.

## Who this is for

- (decision) Restaurant servers on hourly schedules who need to trade a shift tonight; exclude managers, corporate HQ schedulers, and full-time salaried staff.


## Smallest observable win

- (fact) Get a trusted coworker to cover my shift so I keep my job standing and the floor stays staffed.
- (decision) Priority job: get a trusted coworker to cover my shift tonight without group-chat chaos — high importance, immediate trigger, current alternatives leave them dissatisfied.
- (hypothesis) A confirmed cover in one place. Clear who is taking which hours. Manager notified without a second chase. Faster yes/no so they can stop refreshing the chat.


## Current alternative to beat

- (fact) Group chat + DMs, paper schedule board, calling coworkers one by one, or skipping the trade and working tired / calling out.


## What to hide at first

- (decision) No orphan candidates — every listed item maps to an accepted job, pain, or gain.


## Bombs

- Structured confirm reduces group-chat chaos and no-show risk
- Explicit confirm beats group-chat ambiguity for same-night trades


## Blocking unknowns

none
