## Progress they want

## Why it matters to someone else

## Outward pitch

For Cursor Discord builders who ship personal projects into silence. Product-Spine is an installable skill pack: `/product-spine` walks you through a short outward-value grill, saves the decisions, and hands you an honest one-sentence claim you can paste — optional NotebookLM steps from the same notes. Peers get who it’s for and why they’d care, without inventing traction or dumping a feature list.
